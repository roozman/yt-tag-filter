# Privacy Policy for YouTube Tag Filter

Effective date: August 27, 2026

YouTube Tag Filter filters YouTube videos and Shorts in the user's browser
according to tags and preferences selected by the user.

## Information handled by the extension

The extension handles:

- Tags and filtering preferences entered by the user.
- Visible YouTube content needed for filtering, such as video titles,
  channel names, hashtags, links, and descriptions.
- YouTube video identifiers when the optional full-description inspection
  feature is enabled.

Tags and preferences are stored locally using the browser's extension storage.
They are not synchronized to or received by the extension developer.

## Network requests

The extension does not communicate with servers operated by the extension
developer and does not contain analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry.

When "Inspect full descriptions" is enabled, the extension may request the
corresponding video page directly from YouTube over HTTPS to obtain its
description. YouTube may receive the video identifier and normal request
information, such as the user's IP address and applicable YouTube session
information. YouTube handles that information under Google's privacy policy.

No YouTube content, tags, preferences, or browsing information is transmitted
to the extension developer.

## Use of information

Information accessed by the extension is used exclusively to provide its
user-facing YouTube filtering functionality.

The extension's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User
Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Sharing and selling

The extension developer does not sell, rent, share, or disclose user
information to third parties.

The only external communication initiated by the extension is the optional
request to YouTube described above.

## Retention and deletion

Tags and settings remain in local browser storage until the user changes them,
clears the extension's data, or uninstalls the extension.

Descriptions temporarily retrieved from YouTube are cached only in memory and
are discarded when the page or browser session ends.

## Changes

Any material change to this policy or the extension's data practices will be
disclosed through an updated privacy policy and, where required, through the
extension or browser permission interface.
